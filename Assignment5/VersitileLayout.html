The Map

This is Washington, D.C. There are markers for a handful of real-world places around the area: two theatres, one bar, one bicycle-related spot, and three music venues. Each kind of place has its own symbol. Use the checklist in the upper-right to show or hide a whole category, and click any marker to see a short note about that point.
